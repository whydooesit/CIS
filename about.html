About CIS Robotics

CIS Robotics is a FIRST LEGO League team that combines design, programming and engineering to solve challenges creatively and ethically.

Our FLL Mission

We design robots, develop strategies, and prepare presentations to demonstrate our research and teamwork at regional competitions.
